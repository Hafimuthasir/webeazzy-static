What you get

Everything your connected product needs in the cloud

Bring hardware online without building and running your own IoT backend.

Secure device provisioning

Every device gets its own identity and a random credential, issued once during authenticated factory provisioning — never a fleet-wide token baked into firmware.

  • Rotate a device's credential at any time
  • Disable, replace or decommission devices
  • Replacements get a new identity; the old device's history stays separate

Live telemetry and remote commands over MQTT

Devices connect over MQTT with TLS, check the server certificate and sign in with their own credential. Their data reaches you as it is sent.

  • Live sensor readings and device status
  • See which devices are online and when each was last active
  • Push configuration and send remote commands

Staged OTA firmware updates

Upload a release for a product model and the hardware revisions it supports. Device Cloud refuses to send firmware to hardware it was not built for.

  • Type, model and hardware revision checked per device
  • File size and SHA-256 checksum verified
  • Roll out to a small group first, then widen; per-device progress shown

Separate workspaces per customer

Selling devices to several businesses? Give each customer an isolated workspace with its own users, devices, groups and data.

  • One customer never sees another's devices
  • Group devices for monitoring and rollouts
  • Manage everything from one console

Device Cloud FAQ

What is an IoT device management platform?

An IoT device management platform is the cloud side of a connected product. It registers each device and its credentials, receives the data devices send, lets you change settings and send commands, and delivers firmware updates over the air. Webeazzy Device Cloud does all of this for one product line or thousands of units.

Is Device Cloud available today?

Yes. Device Cloud is live. Choose Get started, share your email and mobile number, and our team will set up your workspace and help you connect your first devices.

How do devices connect to Device Cloud?

Over MQTT with TLS. Each device verifies the server certificate, signs in with its own credential, reports its firmware version, fetches its configuration, publishes telemetry and listens for commands and firmware updates. Any device that can run an MQTT client over TLS can connect.

How do OTA firmware updates work?

You upload a firmware release for a product model and list the hardware revisions it supports. Device Cloud checks each device's type, model and hardware revision before assigning the release, verifies the file's size and SHA-256 checksum, delivers it over MQTT and shows per-device progress. Roll out to a small group of devices first, then widen the rollout once it looks healthy.

How is provisioning kept secure?

Every device gets its own random credential, issued once during authenticated factory provisioning and never shown again. There is no fleet-wide token baked into firmware. You can rotate a credential, disable a device, replace it with a new identity or decommission it, and an old credential stops working once the change is applied.

Can I manage devices for several customers?

Yes. Each customer gets a separate workspace with its own users, devices, groups and data, so one customer never sees another's devices.

How much does Device Cloud cost?

Pricing depends on how many devices you run and how much data they send. Choose Get started and we will share pricing for your fleet.
