The short version

  • We collect what we need to run our products, bill for them and reply to you.
  • What you build and upload stays yours. Begone deletes your images right after processing.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Email support@webeazzy.com to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.

Who we are

Webeazzy is a cloud platform company. We build and run Strada (backend as a service), Begone (AI background removal) and Device Cloud (connected device management), and the website at www.webeazzy.com.

In this policy, “Webeazzy”, “we” and “us” mean us. “You” means anyone who visits our website or uses our products.

What we collect

On this website

  • Usage data. We use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site — for example which pages are visited, how visitors arrived and what kind of device and browser they use.
  • Contact requests. When you use “Talk to us”, “Get started” or a similar button, the form asks for your email address and mobile number. We also record which button you used, the page address, your browser’s user agent and the time. This goes to our team’s inbox. A copy is also saved in your browser’s local storage on that device, which you can clear at any time from your browser’s site data settings.

Strada

  • Account details, such as your email address, used to sign you in and manage your access.
  • Project content: the tables, data and end-user accounts that you and your app create in your projects. We store and process this content to run Strada for you.
  • Usage data, such as API call counts, used to run, secure and improve the service.

If you build an app on Strada, your app’s users’ data is stored in your project. You decide what your app collects, and you are responsible for telling your users how you use it.

Begone

  • Images. Images you send to Begone, on our website or through the API, are processed in memory and deleted immediately after processing. We do not store, sell, share or use your images for any purpose beyond delivering your result.
  • Account and usage. If you create an account, we store your email address to manage your access, along with usage such as the number of images processed, which we use for your free images and billing.

Device Cloud

  • Account details, such as your email address, used to sign you in and manage your access.
  • Device data: the devices you register, the telemetry they send, the commands you send to them and the firmware you upload. We store and process this data to run Device Cloud for you.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe, which handles your card details under its own privacy policy.

How we use information

  • To run, maintain and secure our products, and to fix problems.
  • To reply when you contact us, and to help you get started with a product.
  • To count usage and bill for paid features.
  • To understand how our website and products are used, so we can improve them.
  • To prevent abuse of our products.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with service providers that help us run Webeazzy:

  • Google Analytics — website usage insights.
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — the companies whose servers run our website and products.

These providers handle data under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Cookies and local storage

Google Analytics sets cookies to tell visits apart. Our product pages may also use cookies or local storage that they need to work, such as keeping you signed in. The contact form keeps a copy of what you submit in your browser’s local storage, as described above.

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Blocking analytics cookies does not stop the website from working.

How long we keep it

We keep information while we need it for the purposes above — for example, while your account is open. Begone images are never kept: they are deleted right after processing. If you want your information deleted sooner, email us.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email support@webeazzy.com and we will respond within 7 business days.

Security

Our website and product APIs are served over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and Strada tables start private until you open them with access rules. Please keep your API keys safe: Strada secret keys (strada_sk_) belong only on servers, never in browser or mobile app code.

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a security problem, please tell us at once by email.

Children

Webeazzy is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page, and we will post a notice on our website for significant changes. Continued use of Webeazzy after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Questions?

Email us and we will respond within 7 business days.
